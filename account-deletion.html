Request Account Deletion

Owners and staff who want to request deletion of their Bride in Dream Salon app account can contact the salon's support team.

How to submit a request

  1. Email brideindream.support@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address associated with your account and whether you are an owner or staff member. Do not send your password or verification code.
  3. The support team will verify that the request comes from the account holder and explain the next steps.

What may be deleted or retained

After verification and approval under applicable requirements, the salon can delete or de-identify account profile and authentication information. Certain appointment, sales, payroll, or other business records may need to be retained for legitimate legal or accounting obligations; access to retained records should be restricted.

Questions

Contact brideindream.support@gmail.com.

Publication check: The salon must be able to receive and fulfill these requests. If the app allows users to create accounts within the app, Google Play may also require an in-app way to initiate deletion.
