Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy describes how the Bride in Dream Salon management application handles information when used by authorized salon owners and staff. For privacy questions, contact brideindream.support@gmail.com.

Information processed

Depending on the account and features used, the app may process names, email addresses, staff identifiers, account roles, login and verification information, staff schedules, appointment details, customer contact information and service history, inventory records, sales and commission records, and operational activity. Passwords should be stored as secure hashes rather than readable text.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, verify email addresses, manage appointments and salon operations, administer staff access, maintain business records, and respond to support and account requests.

Storage and service providers

The application communicates with a hosted backend and database. Email verification and account-related messages may be processed by an email service provider. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the system. The salon should verify its current hosting and email-provider arrangements before publishing this policy.

Sharing

Information is accessible to authorized users according to their roles and may be shared with service providers solely to operate the system. This policy does not authorize selling personal information.

Security

Access controls, authentication, and reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect data. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained only as necessary for salon operations, legitimate business needs, and applicable legal obligations. Users may request account deletion using the Account Deletion page. Some transaction or business records may need to be retained where legally required.

Your requests

To ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, email brideindream.support@gmail.com. The salon may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or applicable requirements. The updated effective date will appear on this page.

Before publication: Confirm the actual data collected, provider disclosures, retention periods, and deletion process match the deployed app and salon practices.
